# bar.md — quality bar

Bar: **Palantir Ontology page** — clean, but rendered in **white + soft-black only (NO lavender)**.

Checkable mechanisms:

1. **Persistent white header.** A full-width white top header, clear at all times: logo left, a few quiet nav links, one solid dark "Get Started" button (+ minimal icons) right. Optional hairline sub-nav row. Same header on every page (consistent).

2. **Hero lives inside a large rounded rectangle.** A big rounded-corner panel with a white gutter around it. The panel is a **soft near-black** (~#16151c, not harsh #000) on a white page. Content is centered inside it.

3. **Two tones only: white + soft-black.** Page/header white (~#fff), text/ink soft-black (~#141319). Light text on the dark panel. No third hue, no lavender. One dark accent, used calmly.

4. **Huge display title, clean grotesk.** Product word large (cap height ~1/5–1/4 of the panel height), medium/semibold humanist grotesk — never ultra-bold "vibecoded" black.

5. **Small, obvious aperture centerpiece.** A clearly-readable camera-iris (line-art blades) — small, above/behind the title. Not a big ambiguous node cloud. Thin strokes, calm slow motion.

6. **Input sits high, grouped with the title.** The GitHub/URL box is near the title/tagline (upper-middle of the panel), not pinned to the bottom.

7. **Calm whitespace + slow motion.** Generous quiet space; any animation ≥ ~600ms, no bounce/flash. Clean, not edgy.

8. **Prose pages are human + professional.** Mission/purpose page reads as confident forward prose (small businesses, the rise of AI answer engines, GEO vs SEO), not marketing fluff — clear type hierarchy, generous line length.
